Elite Event Hub · Design System

Everything orbits the event.

This is the system behind the layout you approved. Light canvas, gold accent, the Command Center disc anchoring an orbit of modules, a dark instrument strip across the top and a floating dock at the bottom. Every pixel below is live CSS driven by tokens — nothing here is a picture.

00 · Read first

What this file is for

It is the specification. Claude Code reads the CSS in this file's <style> block and builds Blade components that emit exactly this markup. When something looks wrong later, you diff against this page — not against a screenshot.

Law 01

One light source

Gold marks three things per screen: the primary action, where you are, and the one number that matters. In your current build it is on ten things, which means it is on none.

Law 02

Distance is relevance

Five modules you touch daily sit on the inner arc. Structure sits further out and renders smaller. Angles never change between events, so muscle memory carries.

Law 03

Light is home

Planning, budgets, documents and anything printed live in light. Dark is a mode you enter on show days — the whole product flips on one attribute.

Law 04

Numbers are typography

Every figure uses a monospaced tabular face, so JD 214,498 and JD 98,700 line up and can be compared without reading them.

Law 05

Stillness is a feature

Two things loop in the entire system: the overdue pulse and the live-phase breathe. Both mean "this is happening now." Everything else is triggered.

Chrome is constant

The KPI strip, the dock and the Command Center disc stay dark in both themes. Fixed furniture that changes colour when you switch makes the product feel unstable. They are the anchor.

01 · Foundations

The two golds

This is the single most important rule in the file, and the one most likely to be broken. Gold has two values because it has two jobs, and they are not interchangeable.

--gold #8A6209
Text, icons, labels

Dark enough to read. This sentence is --gold on a white card and clears 5.48:1.

VIP Section label
--gold-lit #E8B84B
Fills, rings, active states

Bright enough to carry dark text on top — 9.16:1. As text on a light surface it is 1.69:1. Never do that.

4

Signal palette state only — never decoration

Vitaldone · healthy · paid
Ionopen · in progress
Plasmaplanned · upcoming
Flarepending · watch
Criticaloverdue · risk
Each signal also has two values. --vital for text and --vital-lit for fills, rings and dots — same reasoning as gold. The -lit variants are what you see in the ring strokes and legend dots above; the plain ones are what you read.

04 · Components

Arc orbit navigation

The signature. The Command Center disc anchors the orbit; modules sit on two arcs at fixed angles. Click a node — the active state follows.

How it behaves
  • 01
    Angles never change.
    Venue is always at 3 o'clock, Tasks always top. Across every event in the portfolio.
  • 02
    Inner arc daily, outer arc structural.
    Five modules close in, the rest further out and smaller. The chevron rotates to the next ring.
  • 03
    Counts ride on the node.
    17 open tasks and 2 supplier issues, visible before you click anything.
  • 04
    Positions are computed, never hand-placed.
    Polar coordinates from the core. Adding a module reflows the arc automatically.
